Who we are

A Dubai contractor focused on
one thing: family homes.

Sama Alostoura Building Contracting L.L.C is a Dubai-based building contractor specialising in private villas and residential renovations for UAE national clients.

We manage the full lifecycle of a home — site preparation, structure, MEP, finishing and handover — under one contract and one accountable team. Most of our work is delivered through the Mohammed Bin Rashid Housing Establishment (MBHRE) programme, so we know its stage inspections, documentation and payment releases inside out. That experience lets our clients focus on their future home while we handle the process.

How we work

The MBHRE journey,
handled for you.

01

Consultation & Estimate

Site visit, review of approved drawings, and a detailed BOQ-based quotation across all trade sections.

02

Contract & Mobilisation

Contract signed, permits and site setup completed, programme of works shared with the client.

03

Stage Construction

Foundations, structure, blockwork, MEP and finishes delivered in defined stages with progress reports and photos.

04

MBHRE Inspection

When a stage is complete, the client applies to MBHRE. We prepare the site and documents for the inspector's visit.

05

Stage Payment Release

MBHRE releases the stage percentage. 10% retention is held and tracked against each certificate.

06

Handover & Retention Release

Snagging, final inspection and key handover — after which the retention is released and aftercare begins.

Transparency
Clients see stage progress and payments received at every step.
Retention tracked
10% retention logged per stage until final handover.
One point of contact
A dedicated project manager from start to keys.
